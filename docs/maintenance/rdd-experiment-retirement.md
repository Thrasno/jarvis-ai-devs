# RDD Experiment Retirement

RDD and the canonical 4R review model are excluded from v0.0.0. Their instability and frequent blocking created unacceptable workflow friction, so the experiment is archived rather than shipped.

The historical work remains attributable to:

- Branch: `chore/review-framework-integration`
- Durable archive tag: `archive/rdd-experiment-2026`, identifying the final branch commit
- Draft PR: #445, being closed as not planned
- Related RDD issues: #363, #365, #366, #367, #420, #421, #422, #444, and #461, being closed as not planned

There is no commitment to reintroduce RDD. Any future adoption must be evaluated again from the then-current `master` branch and against a stable, released Gentle AI contract.

Core Hive, Hive API, synchronization, and CLI product work remains independent of this retired experiment.

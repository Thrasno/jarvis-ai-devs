# Standard CRM capabilities

Before custom code, identify whether workflow rules, field updates, tasks, email, formula fields, rollups, validation rules, layout rules, blueprints, custom buttons, webhooks, or schedules satisfy the requested behavior.

State the alternative as advice: "CRM may provide a standard configuration for this behavior; verify edition and organization setup. I will continue with the requested custom implementation unless you choose the standard path."

Do not convert plan or edition checks into a claim that the tenant supports a capability.
